vk_denoise_dlssrr adaptation
---------------------------
https://github.com/nvpro-samples/vk_denoise_dlssrr
Copyright (c) 2024-2025, NVIDIA CORPORATION. All rights reserved.
License: Apache-2.0; full terms are in LICENSE-APACHE.txt.
The Warp/OptiX path tracer adapts the sample's rendering and DLSS guide-buffer
approach. Local modifications translate shader operations to Python/Warp,
replace Vulkan execution with OptiX/CUDA, and extend scene/material handling.
The adaptation used AI assistance.

File-level source mapping under warp_optix/warp_optix/pathtracing:
  dlss_helper.py: dlss_rr/shaders/dlss_helper.glsl (EnvironmentTerm_Rtg).
  ray_common.py: dlss_rr/shaders/ray_common.glsl (mirror matrix and reinhardMax).
  pathtracing_warp_kernels.py: dlss_rr/shaders/primary.rgen, primary.rchit,
    primary.rmiss, secondary.rahit, secondary.rchit, secondary.rmiss, and
    get_hit.glsl (path tracing, hit processing, and DLSS guide buffers).
  optix_programs.py: intermediate optix_programs.h; primary.rgen motion vectors.
  viewer_rt_common.py: intermediate viewer_rt_common.h (matrix and environment
    direction helpers).
  tonemap.py: intermediate debug_visualize.h (debug buffer visualization).
Original upstream notice text is in licenses/vk_denoise_dlssrr-NOTICE.txt.
Sample comparison reference: f8113a84fa952278bb1286e7899f81b7afe8f41e.
Intermediate CUDA/OptiX sources were located in Warp development history at
commit 410d8d71b634e731533a807cb1757066ef2bebe6, under
warp/examples/core/pyoptix/pathtracing/cpp. The Python adaptation was imported
into this repository at aa1c11f0ea6cab9b2a34c428bbd7a1bebc4ad21d.
These are comparison and import references; the original upstream revision
used to start the port was not recorded.

nvpro_core adaptations used by the path tracer
---------------------------------------------
https://github.com/nvpro-samples/nvpro_core
License: Apache-2.0; full terms are in LICENSE-APACHE.txt.
Original file-specific NVIDIA copyright and license notices are reproduced
in licenses/nvpro_core-NOTICE.txt and identified in adapted source headers.
Comparison reference: bc19d6ac3ef62938d0ea0e099735878457ce1b6e.
Source paths below are relative to nvpro_core; local files are under
warp_optix/warp_optix/pathtracing:
  func_common.py: nvvkhl/shaders/func.h (vector, basis, and sampling helpers).
  environment_map.py: nvvkhl/hdr_env.cpp (environment alias-table construction).
  materials.py: nvvkhl/shaders/dh_scn_desc.h (glTF material/texture structures).
  pathtracing_warp_kernels.py: nvvkhl/shaders/dh_sky.h; bsdf_functions.h;
    bsdf_structs.h; ggx.h; pbr_mat_eval.h; pbr_mat_struct.h; random.h;
    ray_util.h; hdr_env_sampling.h; dh_scn_desc.h; vertex_accessor.h; func.h.
    These provide sky, BSDF, material, random sampling, and geometry helpers,
    translated through the intermediate sky_common.h, pbr_common.h,
    mat_eval_common.h, scene_common.h, and func_common.h.
  tonemap.py: nvvkhl/shaders/dh_tonemap.h (color grading and sRGB conversion).
    Highlight compression uses RGB / (1 + max(RGB)), followed by sRGB encoding.
Modifications translate C++/GLSL operations to Python/Warp and adapt buffer
layouts, GPU access, and scene/material handling.
